# Fabrication-first β-Ga₂O₃ rectifier study — Revision 2

**This document supersedes the previous redesign plan and its two planning CSVs for new work.** Historical files stay unchanged. Scope: `15_LeanMesh_Calibration`, including the process-constrained pilots, not just the paper export. This revision is a device/process proposal, not a released mask set, a solver result, or a validated production recipe.

## Decision: keep GaN on the surface, not in the trench

**Primary design:** vertical β-Ga₂O₃ Schottky diode with a single, uniformly deposited, patterned sputtered p-GaN annular extension on an unetched drift surface. Start without an added dielectric field plate. Use one donor stack, one anode metal module, and a matched bare control. Reproduce the published single-annulus process before trying to preserve all of the original design's features. This choice minimizes process changes and directly tests the original research hypothesis: does p-GaN edge termination improve blocking at acceptable leakage and conduction loss?

## What the previous simulations establish

`REDESIGN_V2_RUN_EVIDENCE.csv` is a fresh extraction from native logs and curves. Historical crossing rule: 1 mA/cm² using the legacy 105 µm reference area; 86.59 nA cathode magnitude, log interpolation, five consecutive recorded samples above threshold. The three relocated historical curves match the source outputs by SHA-256. These results are operational simulated crossings, not measured results or evidence of a fabricated design.

- Nominal planar + FP: **2335.90 V**; raised p-GaN HJBS + FP: **2335.86 V**; thin 80 nm/low-barrier planar: **2346.86 V**. All three source logs print global electron `g_e=1.56e-8`. The thin low-barrier case stays a sensitivity result; no hardware optimum inferred.
- Process-constrained **no-FP** planar / raised / shallow trench stopped at **450.98 / 420.41 / 435.52 V** (last complete reverse records), each timeout exit 124. No threshold was reached in the saved data; there is no comparative BV ranking. All three HV logs print global `g_e=1e-13`. Scheme values include 250 nm alumina, 1.58 µm coordinate difference between Schottky and JTE, and nominal Mg 5e18 cm⁻³ throughout the 150 nm JTE. The shallow trench depth is 0.20 µm in code. The pilots are useful initial topology/continuation checks; their tiny currents are not a calibrated leakage floor.
- Old nominal Mg doses (`N_Mg × t`, NOT ionized charge): 7.5/4.2/2.4×10¹³ cm⁻²; the uniform pilot used the inner dose across all zones. The 10 µm drift's nominal donor inventory is 2×10¹³ cm⁻²; neither number proves the actual bias-dependent lateral charge balance. Changing dose, geometry, and g simultaneously means historical and pilot curves cannot isolate any one effect.
- Keep **raw amperes**, fixed projected active-area normalization and, separately, actual Schottky area and termination-inclusive area. The legacy 52.5 µm-radius reference and a 51.20 µm drawn Schottky edge give 8.6590e-5 and 8.2355e-5 cm² before fillets/overlaps. Use measured mesh boundaries and documented AreaFactor for future curves; do not silently use the old 86.59 nA for a 70 µm device.

## Executable research sequence (no solver launch in this revision)

1. **Freeze:** keep originals in place; use the hash-backed six-run evidence as the starter ledger. Derive a same-wafer bare/annulus pair and a process traveler from the selected fab. Resolve anode overlap, annulus width, allowed post-GaN anneal, front/back sequencing, mask enclosure and diced-edge margin before declaring a final geometry. If sputtering/activation or materials acceptance cannot be qualified, choose a qualified Ga₂O₃-only FP process; if a p-type junction is essential and NiO is available, use a separate NiO project split.
2. **Calibration:** preserve the historical bare mesh for reproduction, but fit the new process bare coupon independently. Make film, TLM and heterodiode structures before fitting charge/leakage in the combined device. Qualify n-wafer/contact data first, then GaN charge/contact/transport, then lock calibration before ranking layouts. Use measured data not used to fit as holdout. Match multiple bias/T observables; fitting only the BV is not acceptable.
3. **SDE/SDevice:** use parameterized measured profiles for metal edge/etch taper, passivation and sputtered thickness; instantiate annulus/islands explicitly and label p contacts, insulating surfaces and floating regions distinctly. Keep Schottky boundaries only on exposed Ga₂O₃. Tie electrodes that are wired on hardware and do not short isolated rings. Use `.scm` -> built mesh -> verify region, dopant species, contacts and geometry -> `.cmd/.par` -> verify effective log, carrier selections and nonlocal path. Start with the process planar builder as a scaffold, not a validated target; remove the old zone subdivision in a new revision. Keep `g_e=1.56e-8` in the first matched redesign pair. Improve solver continuation separately, not by silently changing physics.
4. **Electrical gates:** equilibrium and bounded reverse pilot, then checkpoint at matched absolute voltages; tighten allowed voltage increment near a crossing (`Quasistationary MaxStep` is a fraction of the ramp span). Never stop at the threshold if five following samples are needed; use a separate higher bounded compliance. Refine on fixed geometry at least three levels at the Schottky rim, JTE edges, heterointerface, and FP/trench corners where present. Check currents and finite-region fields; a bigger fillet is a geometry variation, not mesh refinement. Preserve signed acquisition order, require terminal-current conservation and bracket sustained crossings; a timeout or plateau is not a BV.
5. **Comparison:** first matched bare vs single-annulus; then same-annulus with/without raised islands; then optional same-stack FP split. Same procured wafer assumptions, projected area and edge clearance, common J/V/T criteria and equivalent contact/process modules. Report leakage at common V, turn-on at 1 A/cm², forward drop at 100 A/cm², differential R_on,sp over 200–300 A/cm² only if reached, sustained 0.1 and 1 mA/cm² reverse criteria (with raw brackets), per-material field extent/location and stop causes. Retain 298/350/400 K as common conditions. Use both normalized area bases so active-ring crowding and die-size costs remain visible. Do not combine NiO and GaN results into a pure geometry ranking.
6. **Robustness:** before process data, use low/nominal/high as *engineering sensitivity*, not statistical tolerances. Obtain CD/overlay from alignment marks and measured CDs; film thickness from maps; charge/compensation from witnesses; measured sidewall angle/radius and etch-damage splits; then sweep charge×CD, overlay×contact overlap, and for FP thickness×fixed charge/plate toe. Azimuthal mismatch requires 3-D or bounding shifted sections, not a shifted 2-D annulus claimed as real overlay. Choose the design that keeps leakage, conduction loss and dielectric stress acceptable across those corners, not the highest one-off BV. No yield claim without a measured distribution.

## Separate the termination effect from the contact process

Chang 2025, Fig. 2 and accompanying text, reports extracted effective barriers of **1.011 eV** (reference) and **1.113 eV** (JTE), and Fig. 3 reports specific on-resistance of **5.51** and **6.15 mΩ·cm²**, respectively. These values describe their devices and extraction, not inputs for our clean deck. Run two comparisons: **(A)** identical Schottky barrier and surface assumptions on the bare and annular meshes to isolate electrostatic shielding; **(B)** independently characterized ordinary and process-exposed Schottky contacts on the same wafer to evaluate actual integration cost. Do not fit the JTE diode’s barrier to its reverse curve and then claim the remaining gain comes from termination. If a faithful GaN-etch exposure cannot be reproduced on a bare witness, document the mismatch and separately bracket the contact effect.

Before same-process charge data exist, treat even the charge bracket as a screening variable, not a nominal material parameter. The depleted drift donor inventory gives a scale, not an optimum for the ring. Sweep net depleted acceptor charge independently of the Schottky barrier and geometry; then cross the narrowed charge bracket with annulus width/overlap. Retain only designs whose behavior is insensitive enough that measured wafer variation can fit inside the acceptable window. Do not carry a historical `N_Mg × t` target into the 500 nm film as if activation and compensation were known.

## What would count as an improvement?

Choose the application leakage limit, reverse operating voltage, allowed forward loss and temperature range before looking at the rankings. Use the published geometry as a replication target, not the old 2.34 kV as a fit target. An unplated GaN annulus advances if, compared with both bare controls, it reduces the Schottky-rim field at the same bias without simply moving excessive stress to its outer edge, and if its reverse leakage and forward drop meet the predeclared limits throughout the supported process window. Equal nominal BV with a worse worst-case corner is not a design win. A ranking reversal across plausible contact and charge bounds is a result: report that the ranking is unresolved and identify which measurement would resolve it.

To avoid treating a simulation fit as a measurement, label each curve as **electrostatic screening** (charge bracket only), **electrical prediction within a calibrated domain**, or **extrapolation**. Do not use high-voltage leakage or avalanche terms fitted outside their validation domain as a hard ranking criterion. For calibration and validation, use separate devices or temperature/bias bands where feasible; do not call the same curve both the fit and validation. Publish a mechanism only when a matched control discriminates it, not because a linearized plot or one field maximum is consistent with it.

## First mask: release checklist

Reserve die space for an ordinary bare SBD, a process-matched bare witness, and a uniform annular JTE SBD. Use the same drawn projected anode footprint in the three cells and map multiple devices across the wafer. Include film, contact, and heterodiode coupons. Before tape-out, measure or obtain the nominal annular width and metal overlap; subtract the worst credible relative overlay and inward edge biases to get minimum contact overlap, and independently check maximum encroachment into the central opening. Do not use the mesh spacing as a lithographic tolerance. Verify film phase/continuity, p-type electrical response, metal contact behavior, Schottky-window residue/damage and the foundry thermal/metal rules. Compare the annulus against **both** bare controls at common reverse bias and temperature, and report a gain only if it survives matched contact/process assumptions, charge bounds, and fixed-geometry refinement. This is a research-device release gate, not a production qualification.

**Next smallest decisive experiment:** characterize or reproduce the published uniform p-GaN annulus on a matched bare wafer split. In simulation, complete the same-wafer bare/annulus pair with verified contact topology, same reference g, measured or explicitly bracketed charge, and checks at common bias. No 90-deck rerun, field plate, low-barrier hero, or trench substitution until this pair passes.
