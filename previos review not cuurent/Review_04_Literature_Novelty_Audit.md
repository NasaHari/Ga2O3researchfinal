## 5. Unsupported or overstated interpretations and recommended literature edits

1. **Narrow the Introduction's premise.** Absence of a readily usable shallow p-type acceptor motivates a foreign p-type semiconductor for a **p–n-junction termination**; it does not mean every edge termination requires another semiconductor. Contrast plain field plates (Konishi), p-type III-nitride rings with FP (Roy), p-GaN JBS/ring/JTE simulations (Nandi, Mudiyanselage, Than and Chang 2023), then fabricated p-GaN JTE/HJBS [4,5]. Reserve the contribution sentence for the controlled aspects of this architecture/criterion comparison. **[Inference based on the cited prior work.]**
2. **Make 400 K the differentiator, not the invention of temperature analysis.** Show which numerical current branch each threshold samples. “Schottky electron leakage” is supported by the device's terminal partition; “dominated by thermionic emission” is stronger than that evidence. In comparable material systems Li reports competing TE/tunneling, and Chang [4] obtains trap-related leakage fits for its fabricated rectifier. Do not import those mechanisms wholesale into the project. Retain “simulated sustained hole-majority rise” where supported and treat its microscopic/hardware interpretation separately.
3. **Fix the high-value parameter attributions.** Split measured, theoretical, empirical and assumed entries in a compact source column. Cite Fiedler for the (001) **normal**, Ghosh and Singisetti for the exact **electron x** coefficient pair, a source chain for the two GaN pairs including scope, and Wei for band-offset context. Do not present the nominal Mg activation fractions, oxide charge or the two tunneling fit knobs as independently measured properties. No coefficients need to change to correct their attribution.
4. **Keep the comparison table genuinely descriptive.** Under the printed source values, recalculation gives Su `(2340²/0.64)/10⁶=8.556 MV`, BFOM `1.033 GW/cm²`; Chang `(3000²/0.8)/10⁶=11.25 MV`, BFOM `1.463 GW/cm²`. These agree with Table III rounding; the first metric is derived here, not a claim about the source's own ranking. Keep the 4.3-kV result as destructive context, mark the unknown reverse convention for Su rather than assume equivalence, retain the Yang author-reported BFOM, and retain the manuscript's fixed area and 1 mA/cm² notation. Do not compare measured hardware to the thin simulated design as though it provided 2.35 kV low-leakage blocking at every specification. Trade-off context is enough; a generic record table is not needed.
5. **Avoid universal claims about trenches or process robustness.** Terms such as “trench underperforms” apply to the tested depth, ring geometry and plate/termination settings; the NiO trench precedent makes a universal claim untenable. Use “sampled sensitivity” rather than “manufacturing tolerance” or “optimal window.” Do not claim a novel breakdown mechanism or that the endpoint's generation maximum pinpoints onset. Reviews 01–02 document the project evidence; neither the literature nor this audit substitutes for fabrication.

## 6. Defensible statement of actual contribution

> We compare three specified p-GaN termination/JBS layouts for vertical β-Ga₂O₃ rectifiers in a common axisymmetric TCAD framework: a planar JTE, a raised-ring JBS with JTE, and a recessed-ring JBS with JTE. For the simulated devices, we quantify how prescribed p-GaN dose and thickness affect reverse-current thresholds and show that the apparent planar–mesa blocking advantage at 400 K depends on whether the current criterion samples electron leakage or the sustained hole-majority rise. We also quantify the loss in stricter leakage-limited voltage for a selected low-barrier, thin-JTE design despite its lower forward turn-on. These are comparative numerical results for the modeled structures, not a first demonstration of p-GaN termination or a prediction of fabricated ratings.

**[Inference] Bottom line:** the defensible novelty is **the integrated comparison and the criterion-dependent interpretation**, not a new JBS/JTE concept, a validated materials model, or a record device. The closest checked papers already establish most of the individual building blocks. The audited sources did not establish the same three-layout, carrier-resolved comparison at the specified thresholds; this is a bounded literature finding, not proof of priority.
