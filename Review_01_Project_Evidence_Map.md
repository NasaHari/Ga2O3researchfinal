## 2. Manuscript structure and claimed contributions

- **Abstract and I, Introduction:** axisymmetric comparison of planar JTE, mesa JBS+JTE and trench JBS+JTE against a selected bare diode; threshold, thickness/dose, temperature, and turn-on versus leakage trade-offs. Published hardware is context rather than a common-criterion league table.
- **II, Simulation Model:** transport/parameters (Table I), mesh sensitivity (Fig. 1), and a common area and metric definitions.
- **III, Device Structures:** bare and plated/unplated terminated designs; three-zone p-GaN termination, active mesa/trench rings, contact layout (Fig. 2).
- **IV, Results:** architecture I–V/resistance and a digitized Yang benchmark (Figs. 3–5 and Table II), p-GaN geometry/acceptor sweeps and field/band plots (Figs. 6–8), barrier height (Fig. 9), temperature and leakage criteria (Fig. 10), and selected design versus published rectifiers (Table III).
- **V–VI:** sampled sensitivities and limits on transfer to fabrication, dielectric failure, contact reliability, traps, self-heating and fabricated ratings. Acknowledgment and references [1]–[9] follow. Novelty/venue suitability are outside this evidence pass.

### Recalculation method and coverage

`Aref = π(52.5e-4 cm)² ≈ 8.6590e-5 cm²`. `J = |cathode TotalCurrent|/Aref`, without another cylindrical factor. Reverse reporting uses `|anode OuterVoltage − cathode InnerVoltage|` on the reverse branch; forward uses external anode minus external cathode. Read quoted PLT dataset names, require a closed `Data` block and complete finite samples, preserve acquisition order, and log-interpolate between positive bracketing currents. Require the high-side sample and the next five saved samples at/above criterion; a five-inclusive sensitivity column is included where different. Extract forward `Von` at 1 and `VF100` at 100 A/cm² (log-current interpolation reproduces stored values); fit V versus J over 200–300 A/cm² by least squares. Compute `Mon=VBR,op²/Von` and `B=VBR,op²/Ron,sp` after unit conversion, not from a compliance voltage.

Of 91 curves, 86 passed record validation (55 reverse, 31 forward); five have an unclosed or missing Data block, and SHA-256 identifies 78 distinct byte streams over all 91 (13 duplicate files). The five incomplete curves are `barrier_height/00_Bare_WF5p10/fwd.plt`, `barrier_height/04_Trench_WF4p90/fwd.plt`, `barrier_height/04_Trench_WF5p30/fwd.plt`, and `rev.plt` in both `pGaN_thickness_doping/2B_Planar_t0p08um` and its `_RERUN` sibling. The selected bare forward bundle is intact. The curve ledger identifies 65 local log candidates (44 with a closing banner in checked tail, 21 without; 26 curves without a local match). A banner, a valid PLT, and a saved TDR are separate checks, not blanket convergence certification. Logs without a closing banner must be assessed against their terminal contact records and exact run endpoints; a staged extract can end without a banner. The thin `_RERUN` log actually trails off in a Newton iteration. Point index in CSV refers to the filtered extraction sequence; Fig. 5 provenance row indices are zero-based raw PLT indices. For reproduction, read the source files named per row and use the equations above; the CSV carries the raw two points in V and A/cm² rather than rounding to paper precision.
