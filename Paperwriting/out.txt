                     III. DEVICE STRUCTURES

   Fig. 2 shows schematic cross-sections of the four representa-
tive geometries. The seven electrical cases are a bare Schottky
diode; a planar JTE with and without an FP; a mesa JBS with
JTE, with and without an FP; and a trench JBS with JTE, with
and without an FP. The trench cases retain the outer three-zone
JTE and are not unterminated controls.

   The drift layer is 10 µm of Si-doped -Ga2O3 at 2.0 ×
1016 cm-3 on a 650-µm Sn-doped substrate at 3.6×1018 cm-3

                          VI. CONCLUSION

   The nominal plated planar and mesa designs in this TCAD
study reach about 2336 V at 1 mA/cm2, compared with
1133 V for the tested trench and 929 V for the selected bare
reference. The pronounced drop from 2336 to 629 V when
planar JTE thickness increases from 150 to 250 nm illustrates
why the termination profile matters. At 400 K, the planar­
mesa ranking depends on the reverse-current criterion. The
80-nm, low-barrier design improves turn-on, but its 925-V
crossing at 0.1 mA/cm2 limits the benefit under a stricter
leakage specification. These results guide comparisons within
the tested design space; they do not establish fabricated voltage
ratings.
